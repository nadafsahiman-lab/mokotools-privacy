Privacy Policy

App: Moko Tools com.mokotool  |  Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how A1 Infotech ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use the Moko Tools Android app (the "App"). The App lets you choose a photo from your device and show it as a semi-transparent overlay on top of other apps that you select. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The photo you choose, your overlay settings and your list of selected apps stay on your device. We do not upload them to any server.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number inside the App.
  • The App uses Firebase Analytics and Google AdMob. These Google services collect certain technical and usage data, described in section 3.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Your photo: you choose it with Android's system photo picker, so the App does not request general access to your photos or storage. Only the image you pick is made available to the App, and it is used only to display the overlay. It is not uploaded, shared or sent to us.
  • Overlay settings: the image location, opacity, position and on/off state are saved locally so the overlay can be restored (for example after your phone restarts).
  • Selected apps: the App reads the list of launchable apps installed on your device so you can choose where the overlay appears. Your selection is stored locally only.
  • Foreground app detection: if you choose specific apps, the App uses Android's "Usage access" permission to see which app is currently in front, so it can show or hide the overlay. This is processed on your device in real time, is not stored long-term, and is never sent to us or anyone else.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The App includes the following Google services. They may collect information automatically, subject to Google's own policies.

Firebase Analytics (Google)
Used to understand how the App is used and to improve it. It may collect an app-instance identifier, device and OS information (such as model, OS version, language), app interaction events (such as app opens and screens viewed), and approximate location derived from your IP address. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)
Used to show ads in the App. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and (where permitted) personalise ads. On supported Android versions it may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising APIs. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

We do not receive your name, contact details or personal files through these services. We only see aggregated reports.

4. Permissions the App uses and why

PermissionWhy we need it
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)To draw your chosen photo on top of other apps. This is the App's main feature.
Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)To detect which app is in the foreground, so the overlay appears only on the apps you selected. Handled on-device only. You grant it in Android Settings and can revoke it at any time.
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)To keep the overlay running reliably while you use other apps.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show the required notification while the overlay is running.
Receive boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)To restore the overlay after your phone restarts, if it was active.
Internet (INTERNET)Used by Firebase Analytics and AdMob, and to open links you tap. The App does not use it to upload your photos or settings.

5. How we use information

  • To provide and operate the overlay feature.
  • To understand usage and improve the App (Firebase Analytics).
  • To show and measure advertising (AdMob).

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share your photo, settings or selected apps with anyone. Data collected by Firebase Analytics and AdMob is processed by Google as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Data retention and security

Data stored on your device remains until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. Data held by Google services is retained according to Google's policies and settings. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Stop the overlay at any time from inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove locally stored data.
  • Revoke permissions (Display over other apps, Usage access, Notifications) in Android Settings.
  • Ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android Settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
